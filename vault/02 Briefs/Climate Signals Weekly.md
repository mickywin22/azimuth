## Regional anomaly map (23 zones)

- The climate-anomalies feed **eased its extremes while staying broadly warm** this cycle: the
  monitored zone count held at **23** for the week to 2026-10-08, splitting **17 warm / 6 cold**
  (from 18 / 5), with severity at **18 NORMAL / 3 MODERATE / 2 EXTREME** — the EXTREME count fell by one
  as the Southern Africa cold extreme eased out ([[climate-anomalies]]).
- The warm side now carries just two EXTREME cells, both cooling at the top: the **Western Antarctic Ice
  Sheet eased to +12.6 °C** (from +13.3, still the cycle's and the brief's warmest regional reading) and
  **California firmed further to +9.3 °C** (from +8.3); below them **Ukraine (+3.6 °C)** and **Europe
  (+3.6 °C)** lead the MODERATE/NORMAL warm band ([[climate-anomalies]]).

  (from −5.9, still the cycle's coldest cell but now dropped out of EXTREME to MODERATE), with
  **Australia (−2.3 °C)** and the **Sahel (−2.1 °C)** the other notable cold readings — no EXTREME cold
  cell this cycle, and the warm field still dominates 17 of 23 zones ([[climate-anomalies]]).
