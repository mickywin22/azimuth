Explore the bundle as a typed RDF graph

Every note in azimuth's public bundle is a typed subject — L1-source L2-brief L3-rule — and every frontmatter field is a typed predicate. That is the whole bundle lifted into W3C RDF through the committed Vault-LD context, at zero authoring cost. This is the concept layer above the knowledge-graph topology: browse the ontology, walk the three OKF classes, open a concept to read its actual triples, and follow the rests‑on edges a static bundle cannot express.

The published graph is self-describing Turtle: schema.ttl (the ontology) · data.ttl (the instances) · Vault-LD OKF profile.
